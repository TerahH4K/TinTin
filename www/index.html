TIN-TIN

A traditional Papua New Guinea children’s game

How to play

Two teams of 5. First, each team takes turns throwing the ball at the stacked cans: tap when the marker is over the cans. The team that hits them becomes the stackers.

Stackers: drag your finger (or use WASD/arrows) to move your child (marked with ▼). Pick up cans by walking over them and carry them to the middle. Dodge the ball! Hit players sit out. Stack all 10 to win.

Throwers: tap where you want the ball to go. Lead your target! If all 5 stackers are hit, the teams swap roles.

Two players: share one screen. Player 1 uses the bottom half and Player 2 the top half (a touch belongs to the half where it starts). Stackers drag to move. Throwers press in their own half, drag to the target and release to throw. Keys: WASD for Player 1, arrows for Player 2.
